Workflow Detective Pilot

You’re part of a 2-week pilot

Help us find the busywork in your week

You don’t need to describe your job or know what AI can do. Work the way you always do. This extension notices the steps you repeat, like copying the same details from one app into another, and turns them into a plain-English list of what could be automated.

1. Work normallyIt runs in the background on 3 days a week for 2 weeks, during work hours only.
2. It spots repeatsRepeated copy-and-paste, forms you fill in again and again, files you move by hand.
3. You review firstYou see every finding and the whole log. Nothing is shared until you choose to send it.

What it records

  • Which work app you’re in and for how long (for example “QuickBooks › Invoice”)
  • Page titles, with numbers and email addresses removed (you can turn this off)
  • That you copied or pasted, and what kind of thing it was (a number, a date). Never what it was.
  • Which fields you filled in (“Customer”, “Due date”). Never what you typed.
  • That you downloaded or uploaded a file, and its type (.csv, .pdf). Never the file.
  • The names of buttons you click (“Save and send”)

What it never records

  • What you type, or the text of emails, messages and documents
  • Passwords, card numbers, bank details, or other sensitive fields
  • Screenshots or recordings of your screen
  • Anything outside the browser, or in private/incognito windows
  • Anything on days or hours outside the schedule below
  • Social media, banking, health portals, and any site you add to the never-watch list

What it records

  • Which app you’re in and for how long (Excel, Outlook, QuickBooks Desktop, your browser…)
  • Window titles, like a file name or email subject, with numbers and email addresses removed (you can turn this off)
  • That you copied something, and what kind of thing it was (a number, a date, some cells). Never what it was.
  • Which fields you filled in (“Customer”, “Due date”), and in Excel which sheet and column headings you edited. Never what you typed.
  • That a file was saved to Downloads, Desktop or Documents, and its type (.csv, .pdf). Never the file or its name.
  • The names of buttons you click (“Send”, “Save”)

What it never records

  • What you type, or the text of emails, messages, documents and spreadsheet cells
  • Your keystrokes. It never watches the keyboard.
  • Passwords, card numbers, bank details, or other sensitive fields
  • Screenshots or recordings of your screen
  • Anything on days or hours outside the schedule below
  • Password managers, banking and health apps or sites, and anything you add to the never-watch list
