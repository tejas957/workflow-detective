# Workflow Detective: Browser Extension Guide

**For:** people who do most of their work in web apps (Gmail, Google Sheets, QuickBooks Online, Salesforce, Shopify, and similar) in **Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge**.
**Time to set up:** about 5 minutes.

## Set up (about 5 minutes)

1. On your work computer, open **your personal link** from the pilot team's email, in Chrome or Edge.
2. Click **Add to Chrome** (or **Add to Edge**). In the store tab, click **Add to Chrome** (or **Get**), then **Add extension**.
   *In Edge:* if asked, click **Allow extensions from other stores** first.
3. A setup page opens with **your details already filled in**. Read **What it records** and **What it never records**.
4. Tick **I agree** and click **Start the study**.
5. Chrome (or Edge) asks to let Workflow Detective **read and change site data**. Click **Allow**. It needs this to notice actions like "copied a number" in the websites you use. It never records what you type or what's on the page. Your personal link page now shows **Added and set up ✓**.
6. Optional: pin the icon so you can see it. Click the puzzle-piece icon in the toolbar, then the pin next to **Workflow Detective**.

**Details didn't fill in?** Copy the code from your email (it starts with `WD1.`) and paste it into the box on the setup page. If you don't have a code, click **Fill in the details yourself** and enter the company code, your name, and the start date from the pilot team.

## During the two weeks

- **Just work normally.** It runs on 3 days a week (week 1: Mon, Wed, Fri; week 2: Tue, Wed, Thu), during work hours only.
- When it's watching, the icon shows **ON**.
- **To pause for an hour:** click the icon, then **Pause for 1 hour**.
- **To never watch a site:** while on that site, click the icon, then **Never watch [site]**. Anything already logged from it is deleted too.
- **To see what it has found so far:** click the icon, then **Open my findings**. Early results get better after the third watch day.

## At the end

The pilot team will do this with you on a short call:

1. Click the icon, then **Open my findings**.
2. For each finding, click **Yes, I do this**, **Yes, and I hadn't thought of automating it**, or **Not really**. Add a note if something's wrong.
3. Optional: tick **Also include my activity log**. It helps improve the tool and never contains anything you typed.
4. Click **Save results file** and email or upload that file to the pilot team.

## Stop or remove it

- **Stop and delete everything:** click the icon, then **Settings and privacy**, then **Stop and delete everything**.
- **Remove the extension:** right-click the icon, then **Remove from Chrome** (or **Remove from Microsoft Edge**).
- Everything it recorded is deleted automatically 14 days after the study ends anyway.

## Questions

**Does it see what I type?** No. It sees that you filled in a field called "Amount", not what you typed in it.
**Does it work in incognito/private windows?** No. It never runs there.
**My icon doesn't show ON.** It only shows during watch hours on watch days. Click the icon to see when it next starts.
