# Workflow Detective: Windows App Guide

**For:** people on a **Windows 10 or 11** PC who spend much of their day in desktop programs: Outlook, Excel, Word, QuickBooks Desktop, and similar.
**Time to set up:** about 5 minutes. You don't need administrator rights or anything technical.

## Set up (about 5 minutes)

1. On your work computer, open **your personal link** from the pilot team's email.
2. Click **Download for Windows**. (This also copies your invite code, so setup can fill itself in.)
3. Open the downloaded file. It's at the top or bottom of your browser, or in your **Downloads** folder.
   - If your browser says the file **isn't commonly downloaded**: in Chrome click **Keep**; in Edge click **⋯ → Keep → Show more → Keep anyway**.
   - If Windows shows **"Windows protected your PC"**, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. This appears because the pilot app is new.
4. A setup page opens in your browser with **your details already filled in**. Read **What it records** and **What it never records**.
5. Tick **I agree** and click **Start the study**.
6. Find the **magnifying-glass icon** near the clock at the bottom right. If you don't see it, click the small **^** arrow and drag the icon onto the taskbar.

It starts automatically when you sign in to Windows, until the study ends or you stop it. It installs itself in your user folder the first time it opens, so you can delete the downloaded file. To open it again later, search the Start menu for **Workflow Detective**.

**Details didn't fill in?** On your personal link page, click **Copy my code again**, then click on the setup page and press **Ctrl+V**. If you don't have a code, click **Fill in the details yourself** and enter the company code, your name, and the start date from the pilot team.

## During the two weeks

- **Just work normally.** It runs on 3 days a week (week 1: Mon, Wed, Fri; week 2: Tue, Wed, Thu), during work hours only.
- When it's watching, the icon shows a **green dot**.
- **Right-click the icon** to:
  - **Open my findings** and see what it has found so far
  - **Pause for 1 hour**
  - Change **Settings and privacy**, including apps or sites to never watch
  - **Quit**, which stops watching until you open it again

## At the end

The pilot team will do this with you on a short call:

1. Right-click the icon, then **Open my findings**.
2. For each finding, click **Yes, I do this**, **Yes, and I hadn't thought of automating it**, or **Not really**. Add a note if something's wrong.
3. Optional: tick **Also include my activity log**. It helps improve the tool and never contains anything you typed.
4. Click **Save results file**. It saves to your **Downloads** folder as `workflow-detective_windows_….json`. Email or upload it to the pilot team.

## Stop or remove it

1. Right-click the icon, then **Settings and privacy**, then **Stop and delete everything**.
2. Right-click the icon again, then **Quit**.
3. Optional: delete the **Workflow Detective** shortcut from the Start menu and the folder `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\WorkflowDetective`.

Everything it recorded is deleted automatically 14 days after the study ends anyway.

## Questions

**Does it see what I type?** No. It never watches the keyboard. It sees that you filled in a field called "Customer", not what you typed.
**Does it read my emails or spreadsheets?** No. In Outlook it sees the subject line of the email you're looking at, with numbers removed. In Excel it sees which sheet and column heading you edited, never what's in the cells.
**My antivirus or IT blocked it.** Tell the pilot team. They'll work it out with your IT department.
