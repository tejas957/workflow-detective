# Workflow Detective: Mac App Guide

**For:** people on a **Mac (macOS 12 Monterey or newer)** who spend much of their day in desktop apps: Outlook, Excel, Apple Mail, Numbers, QuickBooks Desktop, and similar.
**Time to set up:** about 5 minutes. macOS will ask you to allow two things; both are explained below.

## Set up (about 5 minutes)

1. On your Mac, open **your personal link** from the pilot team's email.
2. Click **Download for Mac**. (This also copies your invite code, so setup can fill itself in.)
3. Open the downloaded file in **Downloads**. It unzips to **Workflow Detective**. Drag it into your **Applications** folder, then open it from there.
   - If macOS says it **"cannot be opened"** or **"Apple could not verify"** it: click **Done**, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, and click **Open Anyway** next to Workflow Detective. Enter your Mac password if asked, then open it again.
4. The setup window opens with **your details already filled in**. If your Mac asks whether Workflow Detective can paste, click **Allow**. If anything's blank, press **⌘V**.
5. **Allow Accessibility.** Click **Open Accessibility settings**, switch on **Workflow Detective** in the list, and enter your password if asked. The box on the setup page changes to **Allowed**. This lets it see window titles and which field you're typing in. It still never sees what you type.
6. Tick **I agree** and click **Start the study**.
7. If macOS asks whether Workflow Detective can **access files in your Downloads folder**, click **Allow**. It only notices that a new file appeared and its type (like .csv), never its name or contents.
8. Find the **magnifying glass** in the menu bar at the top of your screen.

It starts automatically when you log in, until the study ends or you stop it.

**Details didn't fill in?** On your personal link page, click **Copy my code again**, then click in the setup window and press **⌘V**. If you don't have a code, click **Fill in the details yourself** and enter the company code, your name, and the start date from the pilot team.

## During the two weeks

- **Just work normally.** It runs on 3 days a week (week 1: Mon, Wed, Fri; week 2: Tue, Wed, Thu), during work hours only.
- When it's watching, the magnifying glass is **filled in**.
- **Click the magnifying glass** to:
  - **Open my findings** and see what it has found so far
  - **Pause for 1 hour**
  - Change **Settings and privacy**, including apps or sites to never watch
  - **Quit**, which stops watching until you open it again
- If the menu shows **Allow Accessibility access…**, that permission got switched off. Click it to turn it back on.

## At the end

The pilot team will do this with you on a short call:

1. Click the magnifying glass, then **Open my findings**.
2. For each finding, click **Yes, I do this**, **Yes, and I hadn't thought of automating it**, or **Not really**. Add a note if something's wrong.
3. Optional: tick **Also include my activity log**. It helps improve the tool and never contains anything you typed.
4. Click **Save results file**, choose where to save it (Downloads is fine), and email or upload it to the pilot team.

## Stop or remove it

1. Click the magnifying glass, then **Settings and privacy**, then **Stop and delete everything**.
2. Click the magnifying glass again, then **Quit**.
3. Drag **Workflow Detective** from **Applications** to the **Trash**.
4. Optional: in **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility**, remove Workflow Detective from the list.

Everything it recorded is deleted automatically 14 days after the study ends anyway.

## Questions

**Does it see what I type?** No. It never watches the keyboard. It sees that a field called "Customer" changed, not what's in it.
**Does it read what I copy?** No. On a Mac it only notices that you copied something and what type of thing it was (text, cells, a file). It never reads the contents.
**Why does it need Accessibility?** That's the macOS permission for seeing other apps' window titles and fields. Without it, Workflow Detective only sees which app is in front.
