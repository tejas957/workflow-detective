Accurate is the share of rated findings the participant marked “Yes, I do this” or “Yes, and I hadn’t thought of automating it.” New to them is the share of accurate findings they hadn’t thought of automating. That’s the discovery the product exists for.

Time / wk is projected from the watch days to a 5-day week, using the time between the first and last step each time. It leaves out reading and thinking time, so it runs low. Results files that include the activity log are re-analyzed with this console’s detector (v).
